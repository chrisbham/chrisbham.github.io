# About This Website

## Thoughtful Classrooms Begin with Thoughtful Design

Classroom environments are more than just the spaces where learning takes place. The way a classroom is organized can shape how students interact, collaborate, participate, and experience a sense of belonging.

This website brings together two complementary resources focused on classroom seating, physical learning environments, and the ways educators can make intentional decisions about classroom organization.

## Our Resources

### Intentional Spaces

**Research, relationships, and the design of classroom environments.**

Intentional Spaces explores the research behind classroom seating arrangements and their potential influence on student engagement, peer relationships, and belonging.

It offers educators practical strategies for thinking about seating beyond classroom management, including how to balance student choice with structure, encourage positive peer connections, and select layouts that support different learning activities.

### Classroom Seating Plan & Arrangement Guide

**Practical classroom layouts and interactive seating tools.**

The Classroom Seating Plan & Arrangement Guide helps educators explore different desk arrangements and consider how each layout supports different instructional goals.

It also includes an interactive seating chart builder, allowing teachers to enter student names, generate seating arrangements, shuffle seats, and print their classroom plans.

## Bringing Research into Practice

These two resources are designed to complement one another.

Intentional Spaces explores the *why* behind classroom organization, while the Seating Plan & Arrangement Guide focuses on the *how*.

Together, they encourage educators to approach classroom seating as an intentional part of creating supportive, engaging, and inclusive learning environments.
