01 THE WHY

THE CLASSROOM IS A LEARNING ENVIRONMENT

A classroom is more than a collection of desks.

The physical arrangement of a classroom influences how students interact with one another, engage with their teacher, and participate in learning.

Traditional rows, collaborative groups, and flexible learning spaces each create different opportunities and challenges. Understanding these differences helps teachers make more intentional decisions about their classroom environments.
