Classroom Seating Plan & Arrangement Guide

Thoughtful classroom arrangements start with intentional planning. Explore different layouts and create a seating chart that works for your classroom.

PART ONE

Explore Classroom Arrangements

Different arrangements support different learning experiences. Consider your lesson objectives when choosing a layout.

01

Traditional Rows

Best for: Independent work, assessments, and direct instruction.

Teaching tip: Keep wide aisles so you can easily circulate and check student progress.

02

Paired Desks

Best for: Think-pair-share activities, peer support, and short collaborative tasks.

Teaching tip: Pair students thoughtfully and leave enough space between clusters for easy movement.

03

Table Groups (Pods)

Best for: Collaborative projects, peer tutoring, and group discussions.

Teaching tip: Angle desks slightly toward the front board so students can remain engaged with instruction.

04

U-Shape / Horseshoe

Best for: Whole-class debates, Socratic seminars, and demonstrations.

Teaching tip: Leave an open centre area for discussion, demonstrations, and teacher interaction.
